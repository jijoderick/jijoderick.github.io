The present parametric study focuses on the mixed convection conjugate heat transfer in a differentially heated lid-driven skew cavity with a thick bottom wall. The bottom wall of the solid is isothermally heated. An in-house solver based on the SIMPLE algorithm is developed in the OpenFOAM environment to solve the governing equations using the Finite Volume Method.
