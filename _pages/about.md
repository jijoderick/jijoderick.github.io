I am a Postdoctoral research fellow at the [School of Mehanical and Mining Engineering](https://mechmining.uq.edu.au/), [the university of Queensland](https://www.uq.edu.au/), Australia, under [Prof. Christopher Leonardi](https://about.uq.edu.au/experts/2881). My post doc reserach will focus of development of fluid solid coupling for applications in energy, mining, microfluidics and biomedicine. I will also working on computational modelling of heart to unveil the mechanics of heart failure and other cardiovascular diseases.

Short bio
======
------
I finished bachelors in mechanical engineering from [College of Engineering Thalassery](https://www.cethalassery.ac.in/), affiliated to [Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT)](https://www.cusat.ac.in/) in 2016. I have received the fifth rank in the university for the same. In 2019, I finished my masters in heat power engineering from [Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology Nagpur (VNIT)](https://vnit.ac.in/) by receiving academic excellence award and institute medal for securing highest CGPA. I continued in the institue till July 2020 as a Junior reserach fellow on a project entitled "Development of fluid-structure interaction solvers to analyse cerebral anuerysm" funded by DST, India. In September 2020 started my PhD at [university of Queensland and Indian institute of technology Delhi, reserach academy (UQIDRA)](https://uqiitd.org/) on ``computational analysis on the mechanics of heart failure with preserved ejection fraction'' <!--under [Prof. Christopher Leonardi](https://about.uq.edu.au/experts/2881), [Prof. Bahni Ray](https://profile.iitd.ac.in/~bray/), and [Prof. Travis Mitchell](https://about.uq.edu.au/experts/25103)-->. My PhD reserach focused on the understanding the underlying mechanics of heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) using continum mechanics based computational methods. I worked on the [FEniCS](https://fenicsproject.org) based in-house finite element solvers provided by <!--my expert colloborators --> [Prof. Lik Chuan Lee](https://engineering.msu.edu/faculty/Lik-Chuan-Lee), MSU, USA and [Prof. Sheikh Mohammad Shavik](https://me.buet.ac.bd/faculty/dr-sheikh-mohammad-shavik) BUET. I defended my PhD thesis on 18 February 2026.
