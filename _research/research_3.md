Contributions
- Modified a computational framework consisting of a finite element (FE) model of the ventricle coupled with a
zero-dimensional model of the closed-loop systemic circulatory system. The framework was built using the
open-source FE solver [FEniCS](https://fenicsproject.org/).
- Developed a simulation approach to model cardiovascular changes at various stages of exercise. The approach is
used to investigate the mechanics of HFpEF exercise intolerance.
- Calibrated the computational approach to reproduce cardiac performance indices of control patients reported in the
clinical literature.
- Investigated the role of HFpEF pathophysiological factors such as myocardial stiffening, contractile dysfunction,
ventricular remodelling, right ventricular dysfunction and pulmonary hypertension in HFpEF prognosis.
