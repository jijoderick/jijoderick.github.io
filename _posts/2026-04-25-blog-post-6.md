Some random thoughts on a PhD. (views are personal)   
======
- A PhD used to be a stressful period for most of the candidates. The main reason is our helplessness in facing problems. Often, we lack the support which was assured by the higher bodies. But always keep in mind that everything in life has this same nature. Yes, life always has positive entropy. Our role is to solve problems and find ways to navigate through the adversities that come in life. PhD may have a slightly higher intensity. Just imagine that you are driving a car on the road. Vehicles come from the opposite side; some come on our same side. Some have better vehicles than we do. Some overtake us, some are slower than us. But if we focus on driving safely and with a proper plan, we will reach our destination safely. Even in the worst case, even if we get stuck in a block or an accident, it won't stop us from driving, is it? Because we know we need to move on, and we always enjoy the small satisfaction we get in reaching our destination. I would like to elaborate on some conditions in this analogy and share some thoughts on how to tackle it.

- Some have better vehicles than we do.   
 Problem: It is a very common problem in the PhD system that you may be working on a topic which is not aligned with the expertise of your group. Often, you feel that you are out of the race. Your results and publication progress could be way slower than those of the other labmates or some other group in your school.   

Solution: The easy one is to switch the group if you detect it in the early stage, and also, you have a better offer in your hand. But this is not the case for most students. You may have a family, which you may have to partially or entirely support with your scholarship.  
So the first step in such a case is to make yourself aware of this problem. Think of ways to solve it. Rely more on reading, learning techniques and practising skills. Identify the groups that are working on the problems. Try to understand how they are solving a similar problem. Always read. Whenever you get stuck in a problem, and you are unable to navigate. Take a break in the form of reading papers. At least your brain gets satisfied in getting new knowledge. Always make more plans, I mean dont stick to just a 4 objective approach in these scenarios, because you may not be an expert to forecast the risks in each approach. So keep making more plans and facilitate you to try an experiment with a new plan if one in your archery fails. Whatever plan works can be accumulated as an achieved objective in your thesis. Use LinkedIn, ResearchGate wisely so that you dont miss any new work in the field.   
Avoid comparison; yes, this is very important. As we are humans, we always feel the need to do it in our lives. Remember, everyone has a different life. To me, life is a problem-solving process, and everyone is solving different problems simultaneously. So you can't make a comparison just based on what the other guy is solving at the moment; his next problem will be different from ours.
